## Instructions

1. In `task_summary`, describe the requested deliverable, the important source
   materials and constraints, and the current stage of the work.
2. In `what_was_suggested`, retain the important advice and attribute each item
   to the social role that gave it, such as Task Owner, Direct Manager,
   Experienced Mentor, customer, auditor, or implementation partner. State the
   concrete issue each role noticed and the change it wanted.
3. Preserve the sequence of the discussion when it matters. If two roles
   disagreed, record both positions and explicitly say that they conflicted;
   do not silently turn them into a consensus.
4. In `what_was_done`, distinguish the initial work from later revisions.
   Attribute completed actions to the role that performed them and retain
   concrete details—such as mappings, quantities, formulas, file changes, and
   validations—that help explain the present artifact state.
5. Distinguish suggestions from completed work. Do not describe a proposed
   change as completed unless the chronicle says the Task Owner or another
   actor carried it out.
6. Preserve any uncertainty, unresolved assumption, or conflicting account in
   the chronicle instead of resolving it yourself.
7. Use the available detail when it is informative. Do not optimize for
   brevity, but avoid repetition and do not invent facts.
8. Describe only what the task and chronicle say. Do not inspect the artifact,
   judge its quality, recommend another communication flow, or decide whether
   the work should be left unchanged.

Return JSON only, matching the supplied schema.
