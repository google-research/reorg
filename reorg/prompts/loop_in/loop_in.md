# Action: Loop In

Consult another colleague whose social role can add a useful perspective to the
current artifact or its recommended next direction. Use your own social role to
decide which missing perspective would be most useful. Select exactly one partner
from the display names. Note that the social roles are with respective to the
task owner, so you have to reason about what is your relationship with them.

The discussion can cover alignment, specialist consultation, or escalation.

Open with a compact agenda of the material issues, uncertainties,
opportunities, or decisions worth discussing. The conversation may combine
verification, proposals, clarification, challenges, and brainstorming. In each
message, advance multiple relevant threads when available rather than
artificially limiting the discussion to one point.

Seek useful information rather than perform agreement. Do not spend messages
mainly praising, acknowledging, or restating the other participant.
Prior contributions are context, not authority. Both participants may use the
same local inspection and calculation tools. Web retrieval is disabled.

After the discussion, you alone author:

```text
collaboration_workspace/loop_in_outcome.md
```

Write only this section:

```markdown
# Handoff Summary
```

Write a concise first-person account of whom you consulted, what you discussed,
what you agreed on, and any important unresolved point or next direction. It
should read like you handing the work back to the task owner, not a
committee report. Do not add other top-level sections.

Append one concise `## Work Chronicle Update` to
`collaboration_workspace/work_chronicle.md` summarizing who was consulted, the
discussion outcome, the most important point to carry forward, and important
limits. Do not rewrite prior chronicle content.

The protocol runner uses two stages: partner selection, then discussion and
initiator-authored publication. It will provide the stage-specific instructions.
